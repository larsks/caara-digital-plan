# Membership management software

## Free options

- [HamClubOnline](#hamclubonline) -- suggested $250/year donation
- [GiveButter](#givebutter) -- free as long as optional donations are turned on; otherwise 3% transaction fee
- [Zeffy](#zeffy) -- free for nonprofits

## Ham radio-specific

### HamClubOnline

https://www.hamclubonline.com/

HamClubOnline is a club management platform built specifically for amateur radio clubs, with features like FCC license integration, license expiration reminders, club voting, and support for multiple member types and callsigns. It is operated as a 501(c)(3) nonprofit and is essentially free to use, sustained through a suggested voluntary donation of $1 per member per year.

## General membership management

### JoinIt

https://joinit.com/

JoinIt focuses on being a simple, affordable membership management tool for organizations that have outgrown spreadsheets but don't need an enterprise system. It stands out with digital membership cards that integrate with Apple Wallet and Google Wallet, a built-in member check-in system, and straightforward pricing starting at $29/month with a 10% nonprofit discount.

### GiveButter

https://givebutter.com/

GiveButter is an all-in-one fundraising platform whose core features are free to use, with no platform fees when optional donor tips are enabled. It differentiates itself by combining membership management, donations, event management, and email marketing into a single platform, making it a strong choice for nonprofits that want to manage dues and fundraising together without paying for multiple tools.

### Springly

https://www.springly.org/

Springly is an all-in-one nonprofit management platform that uniquely includes built-in accounting and financial management alongside its membership tools, eliminating the need for a separate accounting system. It also includes a website builder that syncs with the CRM in real time, enabling dynamic member portals and directories without any coding.

### CiviCRM Spark

https://civicrm.com/spark/

CiviCRM Spark is a lightweight hosted version of CiviCRM, the leading open-source CRM for nonprofits. It stands out by charging no per-transaction fees on dues payments beyond the payment processor's own costs, pricing per instance rather than per seat, and providing a growth path into the full self-hosted CiviCRM ecosystem if an organization's needs expand.

### Wild Apricot

https://www.wildapricot.com/

Wild Apricot is one of the most widely used membership management platforms for small clubs and associations, with over 15,000 organizations using it. It offers a free tier for organizations with fewer than 50 contacts, and its combination of membership tracking, automated renewals, event management, and a drag-and-drop website builder makes it a mature, well-rounded option for small nonprofits.

### Donorbox

https://donorbox.org/

Donorbox is a lightweight fundraising and membership platform that emphasizes simplicity, letting organizations set up customizable membership tiers with recurring payments quickly and with minimal configuration. It is a good fit for organizations that want a no-frills tool focused on collecting dues without the overhead of a full CRM or association management system.

### Bloomerang

https://bloomerang.com/

Bloomerang is a donor management platform that has expanded into membership management, uniquely emphasizing donor retention and engagement analytics. Its signature "Engagement Meter" scores members from "Cold" to "On Fire" based on interaction history, helping organizations identify which members are ready to become dedicated donors. This makes it a strong fit for nonprofits where membership and fundraising overlap and the goal is converting members into long-term supporters.

### Neon CRM

https://neonone.com/

Neon CRM (by Neon One) is a more full-featured nonprofit CRM that combines membership management with robust donor management, fundraising, and reporting tools. It is best suited for organizations that want a single system to handle both membership dues and donor/fundraising relationships, though its broader feature set may be more than a small club needs.

### Zeffy

https://www.zeffy.com/

Zeffy is the only 100% free fundraising and membership management platform for nonprofits, covering all transaction and credit card fees itself rather than passing them on to the organization. It is funded by optional donor tips rather than platform or per-transaction fees, meaning the nonprofit keeps every dollar collected. It offers membership tiers, automated renewals, digital membership cards, and tax receipts alongside donations, event ticketing, and donor management.
